# Trying Peko: your tasks

Thanks for helping. **We're testing Peko, not you.** If something is
confusing or breaks, that's exactly what we want to find.

**Peko Desktop** is an app for running your own AI assistant, called a
**peko**, on this computer. There is no guide: the app should explain
itself. Tell us where it doesn't.

**As you go:**
- **Think aloud.** Say what you're looking for, what you expect, and what
  surprises you.
- **Use only the app and this page.** No terminal.
- **If you're stuck:** say so, look around the app for a couple of minutes,
  then ask the observer.
- **Your model card** has the values you'll need in task 2. Treat the key
  like a password.

---

### 1. Install the app
Open the download page the observer gives you, pick the file for this
computer, install it and open it.
**Done when** a window says *Welcome to Peko*.

### 2. Connect your model
Follow the app's first step, using the values on your model card.
**Done when** the app moves on to *Name your peko*.

### 3. Create your peko
Give it any name you like and pick a face for it.
**Done when** the app says your peko is awake.

### 4. Talk to it
Send the first message, then ask two more things you'd really ask an
assistant.
**Done when** you've had three answers.

### 5. Set a reminder
Ask it to remind you about something **in 3 minutes**. While you wait, click
on another app or window. Come back when you notice the reminder, or after
five minutes.
**Done when** you've seen the reminder.

### 6. Close it and come back
Quit the app. Open it again and find your conversation.
**Done when** you can read your earlier messages.

### 7. (If there's time) Look around
Find where you'd change your peko's name or face, and where you'd add
another model. You don't need to change anything.
**Done when** you've found both, or given up.

---

**Afterwards**, the observer will ask you a few questions. There are no wrong
answers.
